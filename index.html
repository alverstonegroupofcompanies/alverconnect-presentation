The Challenges

The 5 Key Challenges Impacting Healthcare Staffing

Real challenges. A more connected tomorrow.

01

Reliance on Informal Networks

Heavy reliance on informal networks and third-party agents creates inefficiencies and reduces transparency.

02

Struggle to Identify Qualified Doctors

Sourcing qualified doctors and completing verification can be time-consuming, especially for urgent or specialist requirements.

03

Payment Delays & Uncertainty

Delays in confirmations and payments can affect professional trust and create financial uncertainty for doctors.

04

Lack of Workflow Visibility

No structured visibility into selection, availability, performance tracking, and feedback.

05

Dispute Handling Challenges

The absence of a formal system to address disagreements can lead to communication gaps and operational inefficiencies.

How AlverConnect Works

From Registration to Completed Engagement

A simple, transparent process to connect hospitals and doctors — from opportunity to successful engagement.

  1. 01

    Register

    Hospital & Doctor create accounts and submit required details.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Credentials and profiles are reviewed and verified.

  3. 03

    Post

    Hospitals post Sessional or Full-Time requirements.

  4. 04

    Discover & Apply

    Doctors view relevant opportunities and apply.

  5. 05

    Review & Select

    Hospitals review applications and select suitable doctors.

  6. 06

    Complete & Settle

    Session is completed and payment is settled through the platform.

    Full-Time: Hospital handles interview & employment.
